What We Do

Professional Business & Tax Solutions

From entity incorporation and routine GST compliance to income tax planning, high-stakes dispute resolution, and international accounting support.

01 — Company Registration

End-to-end entity incorporation: Private Limited, LLP, One Person Company (OPC), Section 8 NGO, Producer Company, Start-up India recognition, and Foreign Subsidiaries.

02 — GST Compliance & Advisory

New GST registration, timely return filing (GSTR-1, 3B, 9), 2B-3B ITC reconciliation, departmental notice replies (DRC-01), and e-way bill/e-invoicing setup.

03 — Income Tax Services

Accurate ITR filing, direct tax planning, TDS compliance (24Q/26Q), Form 26AS & AIS reconciliation, and advance tax estimation.

04 — Tax Litigation & Disputes

Direct & Indirect tax defense: Section 148 reassessment, scrutiny, CIT(A) appeals, ITAT representation, GST SCNs, and High Court matters.

05 — Business Compliance

Routine statutory filings, MSME / Udyam registration, Class 3 DSC tokens, Director Identification Numbers (DIN), and ROC secretarial filings.

Foreign Accounting & Bookkeeping

Dedicated international bookkeeping, accounts payable/receivable, multi-currency reconciliations, payroll accounting, and management reports for overseas clients.

EXIM Services & IEC Registration

Import Export Code (IEC) application and annual DGFT renewal, customs documentation, export incentive claims, and international trade compliance guidance.
