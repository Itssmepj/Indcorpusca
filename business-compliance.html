Compliance FAQ

Answers to Common Business Compliance Questions

MSME / Udyam registration gives your enterprise formal recognition and provides access to collateral-free bank loans under the CGTMSE scheme, interest rate subsidies, 50% discount on trademark registration fees, protection against delayed payments from buyers under the MSMED Act (mandatory payment within 45 days), and eligibility for government procurement tenders without earnest money deposits.

We work seamlessly with modern cloud accounting software such as QuickBooks Online, Xero, Zoho Books, FreshBooks, and NetSuite. Your team provides access to digital statements, receipts, and invoices. Our accountants perform daily or weekly transaction entry, vendor bill processing, receivable management, bank reconciliation, and provide comprehensive monthly financial statements (Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss, Cash Flow) and management reports.

Class 3 DSC is the highest level of cryptographic digital signing in India, legally required for MCA company incorporation, annual ROC filings, Income Tax e-filing, GST returns, EPFO filings, and government e-tenders. It is stored on a FIPS-compliant secure USB hardware token and is typically issued with a validity of 2 or 3 years, after which it can be renewed seamlessly with renewed video KYC.

Yes. As per DGFT trade regulations, all IEC holders must electronically update and validate their IEC details annually between April and June, even if there are no changes in particulars. Failure to update the IEC leads to de-activation of the code on the DGFT customs icegate portal, halting export shipments and import clearances.

Every individual holding a Director Identification Number (DIN) must file annual DIR-3 KYC (via web-form or e-form) with the MCA by 30th September. If not filed by the deadline, the DIN is marked as 'Deactivated due to non-filing of DIR-3 KYC', disqualifying the director from signing statutory documents, and can only be reactivated upon payment of a ₹5,000 late fee.
