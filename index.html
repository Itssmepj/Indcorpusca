FAQ

Straight Answers to
Common Questions

A Private Limited Company is the gold standard for startups seeking angel or venture capital investment, ESOP issuance, and unlimited growth scalability. An LLP (Limited Liability Partnership) is ideal for service businesses, professional practices, and closely-held ventures seeking limited liability protection without mandatory statutory audit requirements at lower turnover thresholds.

GST registration is mandatory when aggregate annual turnover exceeds ₹40 lakhs for goods suppliers (₹20 lakhs for special category states) and ₹20 lakhs for service providers (₹10 lakhs for special states). Registration is also compulsory regardless of turnover for businesses engaged in inter-state taxable supplies, e-commerce sellers, and entities liable under Reverse Charge Mechanism (RCM).

Never ignore a statutory notice. First, verify the mandatory Document Identification Number (DIN). Second, identify the exact response deadline (usually 15 to 30 days). Third, assemble relevant books of accounts, ledgers, and bank statements. Connect with INDCORPUSCA's tax litigation team immediately to prepare a robust legal and factual rebuttal before the deadline expires.

MSME / Udyam registration offers collateral-free business loans under CGTMSE, interest rate concessions, a 50% discount on government trademark fees, statutory protection against delayed payments from buyers (mandating payment within 45 days), and preferential qualification for government tenders.
