Business Structures

Choose the Right Legal Structure for Your Business

Each legal entity carries unique benefits regarding liability, taxation, compliance burden, and fundraising capability. Our company registration services cover every structure:

Private Limited Company

A legally recognised business entity registered under the Companies Act, 2013. It provides limited liability to its shareholders, a separate legal identity, and perpetual succession. The premier structure for startups seeking venture capital and institutional funding.

Limited Liability Partnership (LLP)

Governed by the Limited Liability Partnership Act, 2008. It provides a separate legal identity, perpetual succession, and limited liability protection to partners while offering operational flexibility and a comparatively streamlined compliance framework.

One Person Company (OPC)

A corporate structure under the Companies Act, 2013, designed for individual entrepreneurs who wish to operate with limited liability protection and a separate legal identity, maintaining complete control without needing a secondary director.

Section 8 (NGO) & Trust

Established under the Companies Act, 2013 for promoting charitable, educational, or social welfare missions. We provide complete registration, documentation, Trust Deed drafting, Section 12A & 80G tax exemptions, and CSR eligibility support.

Proprietorship & Partnership Firm

Simple, accessible business structures for small enterprises and traders. We assist with Sole Proprietorship statutory licenses and Partnership Firm registration under the Indian Partnership Act, 1932 including custom deed drafting.

Start-up India & Foreign Subsidiaries

DPIIT Start-up India recognition for tax exemptions and funding access, alongside Public Limited Company incorporation for public capital, Producer Companies, Nidhi/NBFCs, and Foreign Company Indian subsidiary registration.
